# Recorded sound effects: credits and licences

Every file here is **CC0 1.0 / public domain**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is
required; it is given anyway, with thanks.

The files were trimmed, faded, peak-normalised and re-encoded as mono MP3s
(bright sounds 44.1 kHz/96 kbps, low ones 32 kHz/64 kbps). `node tools/sfx-embed.mjs`
packs them into `src/audio/samples.data.js` (run by `tools/build.mjs`), and
`src/audio/samples.js` layers them under the synthesised effects.
